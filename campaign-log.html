Campaign Log

A record of actions, meetings, and correspondence taken as part of the campaign against Option 7. Not publicly linked.

Wednesday 2 September 2026 — 4:00pm to 8:00pm
Upcoming
Public consultation at My Place, Midleton — 4pm to 8pm, Wednesday 2 September

Irish Rail have arranged a public consultation at My Place, Midleton on Wednesday 2 September from 4pm to 8pm. This follows publication of the new access road proposal, which Irish Rail indicated would be published on Wednesday 19 August 2026.

Residents are anxiously awaiting the publication, in the hope that an option other than Option 7 has been selected.

Please attend if you can, and bring neighbours. A strong turnout at My Place in May made the depth of local opposition impossible to ignore — the same is needed again once the revised proposal is on the table.

August 2026
Update — Irish Rail
Irish Rail request access to local land for an ecology review

Irish Rail requested access to local land in order to carry out an ecology review.

In the course of that conversation, Irish Rail mentioned that the new access road proposal would be published on Wednesday 19 August 2026, and that a public consultation has been arranged for My Place, Midleton on Wednesday 2 September from 4pm to 8pm.

No indication was given as to which option has been selected. Residents are awaiting the published proposal in the hope that Option 7 has been set aside.

27 July 2026
Political support
Meeting with Jerry Buttimer TD, Cllr. Anthony Barry, Cllr. Rory Cocking and Noel McCarthy TD

Local residents met with Jerry Buttimer TD (Minister of State for Charities, Gaeltacht and the Islands & Rural Transport), Cllr. Anthony Barry, Rory Cocking (Parliamentary Assistant and Cork County Councillor for the East Cork Municipal District) and Noel McCarthy TD (Fine Gael, Cork East).

All were committed to support the campaign. They planned to meet with Irish Rail CARC project leaders to emphasise the unsuitability of our residential area to industrial traffic

22 June 2026
Political support
Meeting with Cllr. Anthony Barry, Cllr. Rory Cocking and Noel McCarthy TD

Local residents met with Cllr. Anthony Barry, Rory Cocking (Parliamentary Assistant and Cork County Councillor for the East Cork Municipal District) and Noel McCarthy TD (Fine Gael, Cork East). They walked the proposed Option 7 route with locals.

19 June 2026 — Consultation deadline
Milestone
PC2 consultation closes — Irish Independent publishes coverage of campaign

The Irish Rail PC2 public consultation on the depot access road closed today. On June 15th, the Irish Independent published an article on the campaign: Residents worried about preferred depot access route in Cork rail access plan.

Thank you to every resident, neighbour, supporter, journalist, and public representative who sent a submission, attended a meeting, shared the website, or stood with us at Cotter's Gate. The campaign continues — Irish Rail's revised Multi-Criteria Assessment and the Railway Order stage are still ahead, and the case against Option 7 has only grown stronger.

Up to 19 June 2026
Submissions
Residents sent formal submissions to Irish Rail

Residents and supporters submitted formal objections to Irish Rail throughout the consultation period, sent to CACR@irishrail.ie and via the email tool that was hosted at no2option7.org. By the 10 June online meeting Irish Rail had received around 800 emails from the public, with further submissions in the final week.

10 June 2026 — evening
Online meeting
Online public meeting with Irish Rail and Roughan & O'Donovan — over 20 people attended

Irish Rail and Roughan & O'Donovan hosted an online public meeting as part of the PC2 consultation. The engineers presented a full overview of Phase 2 — new stations, bridges, and associated works across the scheme.

Over 20 people attended. Residents had approximately one hour to ask questions. Almost every question — all but one — was about the depot access road. The project team received around 800 emails to date from the public.

9 days to go until the deadline. We would love to reach 1,000 submissions — please encourage everyone you know to email CACR@irishrail.ie or use the easy email template at no2option7.org before 19 June.

5 June 2026
Update — Irish Rail
Irish Rail confirms it is revising the Multi-Criteria Assessment and depot access road design

Irish Rail confirmed that it is revising the Multi-Criteria Assessment and the depot access road design in light of points raised by the local community.

Option 7 has not been withdrawn — Irish Rail has given no date for when the revised assessment will be available.

Our objections are being heard. Keep the pressure on — the consultation closes 19 June 2026.

27 May 2026
Public consultation
Residents met Roughan & O'Donovan at My Place — PC2 public consultation
