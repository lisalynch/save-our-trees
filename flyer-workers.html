To Irish Rail Workers

Have Your Say on the Depot Access Road

The new Rail Depot at Ballyrichard, Carrigtwohill
Your route to work matters to Ballyrichard residents
⏰ Object to Option 7 before Friday 19 June 2026

What Option 7 means for your daily commute

Roughan & O'Donovan engineers, contracted by Irish Rail, assessed 8 access road options and rated Option 7 highest — but only by a narrow margin. If Option 7 is chosen, staff driving to the depot from the west must travel into Midleton, out to Waterrock, through Ballyrichard, then to the car park on the Carrigtwohill side of the depot. The Waterrock level crossing is also expected to close, adding further congestion to Midleton and Waterrock at every shift change.

The harm Option 7 causes

Option 7 is the only option that requires the destruction of residential front gardens and mature native trees in Ballyrichard. Residents here never objected to the depot. We accepted the night works, road closures and disruption of Phase 2 rail construction — because we believe in public transport. We strongly object to one decision: routing a staff-only access road through our front gardens when better alternatives were assessed and are available.
